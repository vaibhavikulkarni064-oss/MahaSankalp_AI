About MahaCareer AI

MahaCareer AI helps students reflect on their interests and skills and suggests careers to explore. It uses a visible rule-based scoring system, not a trained machine-learning model.

Limitations

Results are preliminary. They do not predict jobs, salaries, admissions or success. Use them as one input alongside advice from teachers, family and counsellors.

Privacy

We store your form answers and generated report, linked to a random session cookie in your browser (no account, no email). We keep only what is needed to show your reports. You can delete any report, or all of them, from My Reports. Do not enter phone numbers or addresses.
